RePosture is a posture-habit reminder. It does not diagnose or treat scoliosis or any medical condition, and its angles are the tilt of the device compared with your calibrated posture, not a measurement of spinal curvature. If you have back pain or a diagnosed spinal condition, ask your doctor or physiotherapist which settings suit you.

Connect with Wi-Fi

Your device must be in Wi-Fi mode.

Device hotspot: join the Wi-Fi network RePosture-XXXX (password reposture) on your phone, then use 192.168.4.1.

Home Wi-Fi: if you saved your network on the device, keep your phone on the same network and use reposture.local or the IP address shown in the device's serial monitor.

Calibrate your posture

Sit or stand up straight in the posture you want to keep. After you tap start, hold still until the device gives one long buzz.
